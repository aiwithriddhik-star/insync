Problem Statements

PS1: Women’s Healthcare

Design and build a digital solution that addresses challenges in women’s healthcare, such as gaps in
diagnosis, symptom tracking, health awareness, or access to meaningful clinical insights. Develop an
innovative solution that empowers women to better understand and manage their health.

PS2: Women’s Safety

Women face a range of safety challenges in both physical and digital environments, from harassment
and unsafe situations to privacy breaches, online threats, and identity misuse. Design and build an
innovative solution that addresses these challenges and enables women to protect themselves, seek
assistance, or prevent potential harm in ways that are effective, practical, and sensitive to their safety
and privacy.
