## Problem statements
- **PS1: Women's Healthcare.** Build a digital solution for gaps in diagnosis, symptom tracking, health awareness, or access to meaningful clinical insights. It should help women understand and manage their health.
- **PS2: Women's Safety.** Cover physical and digital threats: harassment, unsafe situations, privacy breaches, online threats, identity misuse. Help women protect themselves, get help, or prevent harm. It must be practical and sensitive to safety and privacy.

Each team picks one. We work on PS1 first → [[01 - PS1 Healthcare - Pretrained Models]], [[02 - PS1 Healthcare - Datasets & Papers]], [[03 - Reference Material Review]].

## What this means for the build
- **Working beats impressive.** Mock-ups and features that are only proposed get no functionality marks. One coherent end-to-end flow is worth more than many half-built features.
- Use **pretrained models through APIs or Hugging Face** rather than training from scratch. Third-party models, datasets and code are allowed if the licence permits, and **must be credited** (put this in the README and a PPT slide).
- Reference repos such as Femora, vSafe and WomenShield are for **inspiration only**. Forking one and presenting it as your work counts as plagiarism.
- Commit early and often so the history shows the work was done during the event. Freeze the repo before 9 PM.
- The demo video should walk through one realistic user scenario from start to finish.
